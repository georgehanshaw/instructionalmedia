CCSD 573, Week 7

Disclosure, access, and self‑determination

Career counselors help students with disabilities plan, prepare, and advocate for themselves. Work through three activities: reframe statements using the social model, explore when a student might disclose, and practice your own responses.

Medical model or social model?

The medical model locates disability in a person's impairment. The social model locates it in barriers created by environments and attitudes. Decide which model each statement reflects.

    When might a student disclose?

    Whether, when, and how to disclose a disability to an employer is the student's decision. Select each point on the path to compare the options.

    Under Title I of the ADA, covered employers provide reasonable accommodations to qualified employees with disabilities. The Job Accommodation Network (JAN) offers free guidance for students, counselors, and employers.

    Practice your response

    These scenarios use fictional students. Choose the response you would give, then read the feedback.
