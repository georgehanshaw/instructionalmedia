Holland's hexagon and Super's rainbow

Two theories, two pictures. Holland asks what fits; Super asks where you are in your life story. Use both diagrams below, then try them out with your own Strong Interest Inventory results or your case study student's.

Build a Holland code

Select up to three types in order, from most to least like you (or your student). Select a type again to remove it. Use Tab and Enter or Space to choose with a keyboard.

Choose a type

Each type describes a cluster of interests, values, and preferred activities. Most people resemble a combination of two or three.

Consistency

Pick two types to see how consistent your first two letters are.

Check congruence with an environment

Congruence is the fit between a person's type and an environment's type. Choose an example environment to compare its first letter with yours. The codes here are illustrative; look up actual codes in O*NET OnLine.
