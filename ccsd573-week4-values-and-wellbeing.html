CCSD 573, Week 4

What matters to you at work?

Sort twenty work values, choose your top five, and see where they might pull against each other. Then check in on the five elements of wellbeing. This is the same kind of values card sort you can use with your case study student.

Step 1: Sort the cards

For each value, choose how much you value it in your work. Go with your first reaction. You can move a card later by selecting it in a pile.

Step 2: Choose your top five

Select five values in order of importance, starting with the most important. Select a value again to remove it.

    Step 3: Reflect

    The five elements of wellbeing

    Rath and Harter (2010) describe five interconnected elements of a thriving life. Rate each from 0 (struggling) to 10 (thriving) as it is for you right now.
