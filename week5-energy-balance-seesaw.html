Part 1 · Energy Balance Seesaw

Body weight is influenced by the energy you take in and the energy you use. Adjust the controls to see how a typical day’s energy in and out compare. This is a teaching model, not a personal recommendation.












Part 2 · Where Does the Energy Go?

Total energy expenditure has four parts: resting metabolism, the thermic effect of food (energy used to digest and process food, about 10% of intake), everyday movement, and exercise.

Weekly activity check

General adult guidance: at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week (vigorous minutes count double), plus muscle-strengthening on 2 or more days. Any movement is better than none.
