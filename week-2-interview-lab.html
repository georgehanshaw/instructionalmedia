Interview Lab: Interviewing with Intention

Question a witness, collect the facts, and turn your interview notes into a clear report narrative.

Your mission

On a Saturday evening, a witness called 911 after seeing a man break into a pickup truck in a grocery store parking lot. You are the responding officer. Over six rounds you will ask one question each to cover the 5 W’s and 1 H (who, what, when, where, why, how). How you ask decides what you learn.

○ Open-ended
Invites a full description in the witness’s own words.
■ Closed
Can be answered with “yes,” “no,” or one word.
➤ Leading
Suggests the answer you expect to hear.
